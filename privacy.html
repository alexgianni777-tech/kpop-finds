Privacy

What happens when you visit K‑Pop Finds or follow a product link.

On this site

K‑Pop Finds does not have user accounts or first-party analytics. The merch finder runs in your browser. The hosting provider may process technical request information needed to serve the site.

External images and media links

Some artist photographs are loaded from Wikimedia Commons under the licenses identified on our photo credits page. When your browser loads an externally hosted image, Wikimedia may receive normal technical request information such as your IP address and user agent. YouTube videos are linked rather than embedded, so YouTube is contacted only when you choose to open a video link.

Advertising

K‑Pop Finds uses Google AdSense code for advertising. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers or other local storage to deliver, measure and protect ads. Depending on location and consent choices, ads may be personalised or non-personalised. Where consent is required, Google’s consent tools or another Google-certified consent management platform should be used before personalised advertising is served.

Affiliate links

Some Amazon links are paid links. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Amazon and other linked stores process visits and purchases under their own privacy notices. K‑Pop Finds does not receive your payment details.

Independent site

Artist names and brand names are used to identify the fandoms and merchandise discussed. K‑Pop Finds is not an official store and does not claim affiliation with the artists, labels, Netflix or retailers named in the guides.

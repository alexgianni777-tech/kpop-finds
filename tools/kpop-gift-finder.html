How to avoid the most common K-pop gift mistakes

Match the fandom first

  • Confirm the group or artist.
  • If possible, ask for a favourite member or bias.
  • For albums, check the exact version and inclusions.

Then verify the listing

  • Marketplace titles saying “official” are not proof by themselves.
  • For light sticks, verify version and current compatibility.
  • For apparel, check the current size chart and return terms.

ILLIT merch & gift guide

ILLIT's 2026 catalog includes album editions, live merchandise and the DEARLIT character line, which gives gift buyers several good routes without relying on random unofficial bundles. Start with the official Weverse store and use marketplace search only after you know the product family.

Amazon links below are paid marketplace searches. “Official” in a seller title is not enough on its own; compare against Weverse before buying branded merchandise.

Albums

ILLIT has multiple recent releases and editions. In 2026, official Weverse pages include the fourth mini album Mamihlapinatapai as well as the Japanese single I Got Your Back. Match format, edition and member-specific version before buying for a collector.

ILLIT buying checklist

  1. Check the official Weverse naming first.
  2. Confirm album edition or DEARLIT character.
  3. Ask whether the fan prefers group or member-specific goods.
  4. For event merch, match the exact live collection.
  5. On Amazon, check seller, product photos, inclusions and return terms.

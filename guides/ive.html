IVE merch & gift guide

IVE has enough album versions, MINIVE character goods, tour merchandise and light-stick products that “IVE merch” is too broad a shopping instruction. The safer route is to start with Starship Square, identify the exact product family, then compare marketplace listings only when you know what you are looking for.

What is current in 2026?

Starship Square currently separates IVE into music, merch and cheering goods. Its live catalog includes multiple album versions, MINIVE products, world-tour items and the official light stick ver.2. That makes version matching especially important: a product title can be technically “IVE” while still being the wrong member, album edition or generation.

Best gift route: albums

An album is a strong gift when you know which releases the fan already owns. Check the exact version, member-specific digipack and included photocards. If you are unsure, ask for a wishlist rather than buying a random version.

MINIVE and small gifts

Tour merch

Current official listings also show world-tour merchandise such as photocards, acrylic photo stands, posters, shirts, keychains and bags. For apparel, use the official size information first. For collectibles, check whether the listing is random-member or complete-set merchandise.

IVE buying checklist

  1. Confirm the fan's favourite member if the product is member-specific.
  2. Match the exact album version or light-stick generation.
  3. Use Starship Square as the naming reference.
  4. On Amazon, inspect seller, product photos, included items and returns.
  5. For random photocards or blind items, make sure the recipient actually wants a random pull.

How to choose the risk level

When you know only the fandom

  • Photocard sleeves, binders and holders avoid clothing-size guessing.
  • Keyrings and small accessories are easier when you do not know the room or collection.
  • Check whether branded items are official, licensed, fan-made or reproductions.

When you know the fan well

  • Albums work better when you can check versions they already own.
  • Light sticks require exact generation and compatibility checks.
  • Member-specific collectibles depend on the recipient's bias.
  • Apparel adds size and return-policy uncertainty.
A simple rule:

If the gift has to stay a surprise and you cannot confirm size, member preference or current collection, choose a smaller accessory or storage item instead of the most expensive fandom product.

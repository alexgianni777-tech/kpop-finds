What are K-pop photocard dimensions?

There is no single size you can safely assume for every K-pop photocard, inclusion, sleeve or binder pocket. Measure the exact card’s width and height in millimetres, then compare the outside of the sleeved card with the pocket’s usable inside dimensions. A pocket can look large enough while seams or a second sleeve make the fit too tight.

For a quick fit check, measure the card, the sleeved card and the inside of one pocket before ordering a full binder or refill pack. The calculator below helps with capacity after you have confirmed the physical fit.

How many photocard binder pages do I need?

Count your cards, leave room for your next additions and compare page layouts before buying supplies. All numbers stay in this browser.

Count separate usable slots, not just printed pocket numbers. Use 18 only if the sheet has 18 separate slots. This planner does not assume two cards in one pocket.

Example: 100 cards and 20 empty spaces

You need room for 120 cards. At 9 usable slots per sheet, round 120 ÷ 9 up to 14 sheets. That gives 126 spaces. To sleeve all 120 planned cards with 100 sleeves per pack, buy 2 packs.

Formula: sheets = (cards + extra spaces) ÷ slots per sheet, rounded up. Sleeve packs = (cards + extra spaces) ÷ sleeves per pack, rounded up. Binder capacity is a separate check: rings and page thickness vary.

Photocard sleeve sizes: measure three different things

A size printed on a sleeve pack can describe the card it fits or the sleeve itself. Do not assume the two measurements are the same. Ask for the usable inside measurement when a listing is unclear.

  1. Card: measure width and height in millimetres. Include rounded corners in the overall measurement; do not trim a card to fit.
  2. Sleeve: check the maker’s stated card compatibility, then measure the outside of the sleeved card. A second sleeve adds width and thickness.
  3. Pocket: compare the sleeve’s outside dimensions with the pocket’s usable inside dimensions. It should slide in without bending or force.

A5 ring binder, fixed-page album or toploader storage?

Choose a refillable ring binder when you rearrange sets often.
Pages can move with your collection. Match hole count and spacing before ordering refills; “A5” describes a format, not a guarantee that every refill fits every ring mechanism. Keep cards away from ring pressure and leave turning room.
Choose a fixed-page binder when you want a complete setup.
You do not buy separate refill pages, but you cannot add more when it is full. Compare usable pockets, sleeve fit and whether you can see both sides of a photocard.
Choose dedicated oversized or toploader storage for thicker holders.
Measure the outer holder, not only the card. Check capacity with holders installed, because a thick setup can fill a binder before its advertised card count.

Do not count both sides twice

For the calculator above, enter usable slots per physical sheet. A sheet with nine pockets on each of two separate sides has 18 slots. A transparent nine-pocket sheet with the same pockets visible from both sides has nine slots if you store one card per pocket. Putting two cards back-to-back hides their backs and adds thickness.

A binder sold with a fixed capacity of 360 cards already includes its pages. Do not add refill sheets to your shopping list unless the binder is designed to accept them. For a refillable binder, leave expansion space and check its page capacity separately from the calculator’s result.

Before filling the binder

  1. Test one card and sleeve in one pocket before opening a full pack of refills.
  2. Turn a filled page slowly and close the binder gently. Stop if cards curve, catch on rings or press against the zip.
  3. Use dividers or a separate checklist for missing cards instead of packing pockets tightly.
  4. Keep the binder away from direct sun, damp areas and heat. Check the sleeve and page manufacturer’s material information.

Can I buy a binder as a surprise gift?

Ask for a photo of the recipient’s current sleeve pack and binder refills, or choose together. Knowing the fandom does not tell you the required pocket size.

Do I need double sleeves?

It is an optional extra layer, not a prerequisite for using this guide. Check the outer sleeve fit and resulting thickness first. More layers do not fix an undersized pocket.

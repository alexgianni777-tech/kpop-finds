The five checks that matter most

Before checkout

  1. Official status. Start with the artist or label's official store.
  2. Version. Match the exact generation and model name.
  3. Included parts. Check straps, stands, cases, cables and batteries.

Before a concert

  1. Compatibility. Confirm current app or venue-sync requirements.
  2. Seller & returns. Keep enough time to resolve a wrong or unclear item.
Lower-risk gift route:

If you do not know which fanlight generation the recipient wants, an album, photocard binder, keychain or other smaller fandom item usually requires less technical guessing.

What to verify before buying

Product match

  • Exact album, version, member or character selection.
  • What is actually included in the box or bundle.
  • For apparel: the current size chart and return terms.
  • For collectibles: official, licensed, fan-made or reproduction status.

Concert & seller checks

  • For light sticks: version, app support, venue compatibility and batteries.
  • Seller identity and fulfilment details on the current listing.
  • Whether packaging and naming match the official merch reference.
  • Current price and availability on the retailer page, not copied claims.

Avoid the most common mistake

Do not pay an official-merch premium just because a marketplace title contains “official.” Listing titles can be inaccurate or ambiguous. Cross-check product naming, packaging, version and seller information against the official source before deciding.

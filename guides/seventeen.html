SEVENTEEN merch & gift guide

SEVENTEEN has a large official catalog spanning albums, MINITEEN, light sticks, CARAT LAND merchandise and world-tour collections. That breadth is great for gifting but makes exact collection names important. Start with Weverse, then compare the product you actually want.

SEVENTEEN buying checklist

  1. Start with Weverse and identify the exact collection.
  2. Check the member or MINITEEN character.
  3. For albums, confirm edition and duplicate risk.
  4. For light sticks, verify the current version.
  5. On Amazon, inspect seller, photos, inclusions and returns.

# K‑Pop Finds research snapshot — 2026-10-02

This prototype does **not** claim an exact universal search-volume ranking. The first launch set combines recent attention/search proxies, current chart visibility and active official merchandise.

## Why these fandoms

- **BTS** — June 2026 idol brand-reputation leader; also the strongest group in a recent K-pop attention dataset. Active 2026 ARIRANG world-tour official merchandise on Weverse.
- **BLACKPINK** — high current group/member attention; official DEADLINE and BPCOLLECTION merchandise launched on Weverse in March 2026.
- **KPop Demon Hunters / Golden** — "Golden" ranked No. 1 on Circle Chart's global K-pop chart in July and August 2026; Netflix says the franchise has a broad official product program including Mattel, Hasbro, Funko, apparel and more.
- **Stray Kids** — among the strongest recent group-attention signals and has official JYP goods collections in 2026.
- **KATSEYE** — strong recent attention signal and active WILD-era official CDs, vinyl and apparel in its official store.
- **ENHYPEN** — high recent attention and multiple current official 2026 merch programs on Weverse.
- **aespa** — high current attention and an extensive SMTOWN official merchandise catalog including fanlight products, albums, cards and tour goods.
- **TWICE** — established current attention plus active official store/JYP tour merch and Candybong products.

## Affiliate and image rules

Amazon marketplace search links use the current Associates tracking tag as a prototype placeholder. Before a separate K‑Pop Finds domain goes live, add that domain to Associates Central and ideally create a dedicated tracking ID.

Do not scrape or hotlink artist press photos, album art, Netflix art, official-store product photography or Amazon product images. Use original site graphics until product images are obtained through a permitted Amazon/brand method.

## YouTube / watch-page policy

The site now has one `/watch/<fandom>.html` page per catalog fandom. Each page uses a privacy-enhanced `youtube-nocookie.com` embed for the featured official video already referenced in `catalog.json`. The site does not host videos, copy lyrics or use copied YouTube thumbnails. Watch pages connect the music experience to the fandom guide and three verified products.
